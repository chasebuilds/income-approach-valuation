Limitations

  • This is not an appraisal and gives no opinion of value for any lending, tax, legal or sale purpose. A USPAP-compliant appraisal requires a licensed or certified appraiser.
  • Results are only as good as the inputs. Cap rates, rents, expenses and growth rates must come from verified market evidence.
  • The examples use made-up numbers. The benchmark ranges are illustrative.
  • Models are simplified: annual periods, year-end cash flows, a single rent growth rate, no leasing costs, tenant improvements, loan fees or income tax.
  • Reconciliation weights are your judgment. The tool only does the arithmetic.
